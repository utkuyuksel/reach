REACH is a number puzzle game. You can play without creating an account. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage analytics via Google Firebase / Google Analytics — e.g. which screens are opened and which puzzles are started or completed — to understand how the game is played and improve it. This is not tied to your identity.
  • Advertising via Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier to show ads and limit repetition. Premium removes ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple App Store / Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred; we never see your payment details.
  • On-device data (your progress, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded.

What we do not do

We do not require sign-in, do not sell your data, and do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location.

Children

REACH is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings, and remove ads by purchasing Premium.
